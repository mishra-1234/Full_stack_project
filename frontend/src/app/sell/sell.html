Seller FAQs

Got questions? We've got answers.

How long does registration and verification take? +

Account creation is instant. Document and KYC verification usually takes between 24 to 48 business hours once submitted.

Are there any hidden costs or listing fees? +

Zero listing or recurring fees. We only charge a category commission once an item is successfully sold and delivered.

Can I sell without a GST number? +

Yes, if you sell exclusively in GST-exempt categories such as books, select handicrafts, or unbranded handlooms.

When and how do I receive my payouts? +

Payouts are credited directly to your verified bank account on a regular weekly cycle once your balance exceeds ₹500.

Who manages shipping and customer delivery? +

Bazario partners with top logistics providers. Simply pack your item with the generated shipping label, and our partner handles pickup and delivery.
